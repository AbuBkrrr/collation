On election day, the gap between a rumour and the truth is measured in minutes.

A day in the situation room

How election day feels with it on.

06:30

Doors open, map lights up

Observers log in across the state. Green dots bloom on the map as each polling unit comes online. You can see your whole coverage at a glance.

09:15

A flashpoint, seen live

An observer in Fagge taps Report Incident. Her last sixty seconds of video and her exact location land in the dispatch queue. The nearest patrol is on screen with a four‑minute ETA. You dispatch before it escalates.

13:40

Numbers you can watch

Accreditation closes and figures start flowing in by voice. The collation bars climb and reorder themselves live. You are reading the election as it happens, not after.

16:20

A dispute, settled cleanly

Two observers at one unit disagree by forty votes. The unit turns amber and drops out of the count. A supervisor opens both EC8A photos, enters the correct figure and signs it. The total is right, and the record shows why.

20:00

Results that hold

As units verify, your totals firm up — each one backed by two witnesses and a result‑sheet photo. When you announce, you are standing on evidence.
